Get in touch

Finished pieces, ready to sell

Done by hand, and checked piece by piece.

  • Castings cleaned up and smoothed

    Sprues cut off, and seams and casting marks filed and sanded away.

  • Polished to your finish

    High shine, satin, brushed or oxidised, matched to your reference piece.

  • Pieces assembled, ready to wear

    Jump rings, clasps, earring posts and hooks soldered, and chains cut to length and finished.

  • Whole pieces made from start to finish

    Hand fabrication from your drawing or a sample, in sheet and wire: sawn, formed, soldered and finished. For samples and small batches.

Two ways to work with me

Set days each week

One or two days a week in your workshop, on a fixed schedule.

Batches on request

I collect the pieces in Lisbon, finish them at my bench and bring them back.

What regular help gives you

One less job on your list each week.

  • Bestsellers kept in stock

    Restocks done on a set day or sent as a batch, so the pieces that sell best are back before they run out.

  • Time to design and sell, or a day off

    Less of your week at the polishing motor. Spend it on new designs and your customers, or take the day off.

  • A skilled jeweller when you need one

    I’m self-employed and invoice through recibos verdes, so there’s no contract or payroll.

  • Extra help in busy seasons

    More days or bigger batches before Christmas, Valentine’s Day and Mother’s Day.

  • Finished the way you like it

    Details and finishes done to your preferences, checked against your reference piece.

  • Launch new pieces faster

    Castings are finished the week they come back from the caster, at your bench or mine.

Where I work and train

Cris Maria

When the pieces come back, I take each one from raw casting to finished piece, assembling, soldering and giving it the finish and texture she wants. I also help customers with purchases, carry out repairs and adjustments, resize or adapt pieces on request, and keep the shop organised.

How we’d start

There’s no contract. Try it first, and only carry on if it helps.

Set days each week

  1. Send me a message

    Tell me what needs to be done.

  2. A paid trial day

    I spend a day at your bench on a first batch, and you check the finish.

  3. Regular days

    If the day goes well, we set a regular day or two. You can stop at any point.

Batches on request

  1. Send me a message

    Tell me what needs to be done, how many pieces, and when you need them back.

  2. I send you a quote

    Worked out from the pieces and the time they need. Once you accept it, I collect them in Lisbon, or you post them to me.

  3. Back to you, finished

    I finish them at my bench and bring them back for you to check.

Questions from jewellery brands

Where can I find a freelance bench jeweller in Lisbon?

Right here. I’m a freelance bench jeweller based in Lisbon, working one or two set days a week at a brand’s bench, or finishing batches at mine.

How much does a freelance bench jeweller cost in Lisbon?

I give you a price before we start: a day rate for days at your bench, or a quote for each batch at mine, from the pieces and the time they need. Send me a message with what you need.

Can I have a jeweller at my bench one or two days a week?

Yes. I come to your workshop in Lisbon on one or two set days a week and work with your tools.

Can you finish a batch of castings at your bench?

Yes. Once you accept the quote, I collect the pieces in Lisbon, or you post them to me. I finish them at my bench and bring them back.

What bench work do you take on?

Cleaning up castings, polishing to your finish, soldering and assembly, repairs and resizing, and whole pieces made in sheet and wire from your drawing or a sample.

Can I outsource polishing, repairs and resizing to you?

Yes. I polish to your finish, repair and adjust pieces, and resize or adapt them on request, as I do at Cris Maria.

Are you a jewellery manufacturer?

No. I’m one bench jeweller, so I suit small batches and finishing work rather than factory runs.

Which metals do you work with?

Silver, gold, gold-plated pieces and brass.

Is there a contract or a minimum?

No contract and no minimum. Bench days start with a paid trial day, and you can stop at any time.

How do you invoice?

I’m self-employed and issue a recibo verde for each month’s work.

Will my designs stay confidential?

They stay yours, made to your specs, and I’m happy to sign a confidentiality agreement.

Where have you trained and worked?

Three years of bench classes at Allis Lab in Graça, and I’ve finished cast silver for Cris Maria in Alfama every week since 2025.

Can you look after our shop too?

Yes, between jobs at the bench: assisting customers, selling, answering questions about the pieces, and handling repairs and resizing. I’ve worked at Cris Maria, Toranja and The Feeting Room.

Can you take on a rush job?

Often, yes. Tell me the deadline when you get in touch and I’ll say straight away whether I can make it.

What if you’re fully booked?

The week at the top of this page shows when I’m free. If it’s full, I’ll tell you the first date I can start.
